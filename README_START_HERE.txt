APEXHUB CSC 426 EXAM STUDIO
===========================
Created for revision before Saturday, 14 November 2026, 7:00 AM WAT.

START IN THREE STEPS
1. Extract the entire CSC426_Exam_Studio_Offline_Pack.zip archive.
2. Open the extracted CSC426_Exam_Studio folder.
3. Double-click index.html. Chrome or Microsoft Edge is recommended.

Do not open index.html from inside the ZIP preview. Extract the files first.
Keep the videos folder next to index.html. No installation or account is required.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- 61 focused lessons across all 7 course chapters.
- Full academic definitions + plain-language explanations and Nigerian university examples.
- Replayable animated diagrams (Fishbone, SDLC models, testing, migration and more).
- Listen buttons for browser read-aloud. This depends on voices available in your browser/Windows.
- SIX original MP4 video explainers with spoken computer-generated narration, available offline.
- 89 multiple-choice questions and 46 longer-form exam questions.
- Interactive author/date/definition flashcards and author-matching challenges.
- Adaptive revision: missed questions are prioritised, with periodic recall prompts when the page stays open.
- Timed 35-question mock exam with automatic marking and corrections.
- Editable lesson notes and a 25-minute study timer.
- Local progress saving, plus export/import of a JSON progress backup.
- Complete study-notes PDF (41 pages).
- Complete question bank PDF, with ANSWERS AT THE BACK (34 pages).
- One-page author memory sheet PDF.

RECOMMENDED STUDY ORDER
Start at Overview -> Chapter 1. For every lesson:
1. Read the FULL definition aloud.
2. Try the real-life example and animated explanation.
3. Explain the topic without looking.
4. Answer the lesson question. If wrong, review the explanation.
5. Mark the lesson as studied.

Every three completed lessons, the app offers a recall checkpoint.
It also offers recall checks after about ten minutes WHILE THE WEBSITE IS OPEN.

After all seven chapters:
Author Memory Arena -> Written Practice -> Timed Mock Exam -> Question Vault.
Use the question bank PDF to do an answer-free revision on paper.

WATCH VIDEOS
Choose the Video explainers item from the left navigation. All six MP4 clips
are included locally in the videos folder, with narration and captions displayed
on screen. They are deliberately short refreshers, not replacements for the full definitions.

PROGRESS AND PRIVACY
Progress is kept in the browser storage on the device where you open the site.
Clearing site data or switching browsers may reset it. In Revision Plan, click
Export progress JSON to back it up; use Import progress JSON to restore.
The site does not upload your answers anywhere.

OFFLINE AND BROWSER NOTES
The website, questions, diagrams and MP4 videos do not need internet access.
Browser-generated narration depends on locally available system voices and browser support.
The MP4 narration is embedded in the files and works without online voices.

If your browser blocks saving progress when opening index.html directly,
open PowerShell in this extracted folder and run:
    py -m http.server 8080 --bind 127.0.0.1
Then open:
    http://127.0.0.1:8080
Press Ctrl+C in PowerShell to stop the local server.
